wirdwatcher

You get a random word, and you rate it out of 5.0. Version 2 runs on your own computer with no server and no account. Friends swap ratings directly, and up to 8 people can sit at a lobby table and reveal together.

  • Word of the day, synonym chains, topic playlists and search by meaning
  • XP, levels, 97 badges and cosmetics, with rewards that never depend on the rating you give
